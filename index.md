# Solluna Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 23, 2026

Solluna is a welcome bot created for the Chismaks PH community on Root. This policy explains how Solluna handles information when members join the community.

## 1. Information Solluna Uses

When a member joins Chismaks PH, Solluna:
- Receives a notification that a member has joined.
- Retrieves the member's Root nickname to personalize the welcome message.
- Posts a welcome message containing that nickname in the community's default channel.
- Writes basic success or error information to its developer logs. A success log may include the member's nickname.

Solluna is not designed to maintain a separate member profile database or collect information for advertising.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by Solluna is used to welcome new members and help the developer troubleshoot errors. Solluna does not intentionally use this information for advertising or sell members' personal information.

## 3. Storage and Retention

Solluna's current code does not intentionally save member nicknames or join events in a separate database. However, the nickname appears in the welcome message and may appear in developer logs.

Root operates the platform and hosting infrastructure. Root may process or retain information under its own policies and technical practices. Please refer to Root's applicable privacy information for details about platform-level processing and retention.

## 4. Sharing

Solluna posts the welcome message in a community channel, where it may be visible to members who can access that channel. Solluna does not intentionally send member information to third-party advertising services.

## 5. Security

The developer takes reasonable care to avoid collecting unnecessary information and to protect bot credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Choices and Requests

If you have questions about Solluna or want to raise a privacy or data-handling concern, contact the Chismaks PH community administrators or the bot's developer through Root.

Messages posted in the community may be subject to Root's platform features and policies, including any applicable message deletion controls.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Solluna's features or information practices change. The latest version will be published at the same webpage address.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions about Solluna, contact the Chismaks PH community administrators or the bot's developer through Root.
